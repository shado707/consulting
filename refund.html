Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

At Software Engineering & Automation Consulting, we are committed to delivering high-quality, reliable, and verified software deliverables. This Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines the terms governing project cancellations and refund requests for our consulting and engineering engagements.

1. Milestone-Based Engagements & Verification

Our software engineering and automation projects are structured around defined milestones (e.g., architecture design, core pipeline implementation, testing and delivery). Prior to milestone acceptance and invoicing, the Client is provided with access to test environments, execution logs, code repositories, or demonstration artifacts for review.

2. Cancellation Policy

  • Before Work Commences: If a Client requests project cancellation before engineering work or environment setup has begun, a full refund of any pre-paid retainer will be issued, minus applicable bank or payment processing fees.
  • During Active Milestone Work: If a project is cancelled while a milestone is in progress, billing will be prorated strictly for the hours logged or verifiable sub-tasks completed up to the cancellation notice timestamp. Remaining advance balances for unstarted work will be returned.

3. Refund Eligibility & Request Window

Clients may request a refund under the following conditions:

  • Deliverables materially fail to meet the written technical specifications agreed upon in the statement of work, and the Consultant is unable to rectify the defects within a reasonable remediation window (typically 7 business days).
  • Refund requests must be submitted in writing within 7 calendar days of deliverable submission or milestone invoicing.

4. Non-Refundable Items

Refunds cannot be granted for:

  • Milestones that have been formally reviewed, accepted, and signed off by the Client.
  • Third-party costs incurred on the Client's behalf with prior approval (e.g., dedicated cloud hosting, third-party API licensing, domain registrations).
  • Delays or issues caused by the Client's failure to provide required credentials, access, or essential inputs.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed through our payment gateway provider (e.g., Razorpay) directly back to the Client's original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, or Credit/Debit Card). In accordance with standard banking settlement procedures, refunds typically reflect in the Client's account within 5 to 7 business days following approval.
